A place to sit with a thought.

Soulspasms began in 2014 as a personal creative-writing publication by Ishioma. It holds short stories, poetry, personal reflections and commentary on society.

This revival is intentionally simple. The work comes first. The design exists to make reading feel good, not to compete with it.

This short introduction is draft copy and can be revised before the public launch.
